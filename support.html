Support

Bloomdex identifies San Francisco flowers from a photo, entirely on your phone. If something is not working, or your kid found a flower the app did not know, write to SUPPORT_EMAIL. A real person reads it.

Common questions

The app says it needs the camera

Open Settings, find Bloomdex, and turn on Camera. The app cannot identify anything without it.

It said nothing, or asked me to get closer

That is on purpose. Bloomdex gives one confident answer or none, never a guess. Get closer so the flower fills the frame, hold still, and try again in good light.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Identification, the collection and the narration all run on the phone. The only time it reaches the internet is when you tap the Wikipedia button and confirm.

Narration is silent in my language

Narration for languages other than English downloads from Apple after the app installs. Open the app on Wi-Fi for a minute and it will arrive. English narration is always included.

What flowers does it know?

Flowers that grow wild or in gardens around San Francisco. It does not know everything, and it will say nothing rather than guess at something it has not learned.

My collection disappeared

The collection lives only on the phone. Deleting the app deletes it. Reinstalling starts a fresh collection.
